## 1. Summary

Recent reports of **new radio signals from Europa** (Jupiter's moon) have been investigated and brought into the **3I/ATLAS** capture campaign and **Space Cloud** assets. Europa is assigned a role, included in the Three-eye Atlas campaign, and is **tracking for now**.

---

## 2. Relation to 3I/ATLAS and Sun Spots

- **Same Jupiter system.** 3I/ATLAS executes the March 16 **Jupiter Hill Radius lock**; Europa is a moon of Jupiter. Any Jovian-system radio (including Europa–Jupiter interaction) shares the same gravitational and electromagnetic neighborhood.
- **Sun spots.** Space Cloud already uses **Solar AR4379** (M-flare probability) and coronal / ionospheric context. Europa-reported "new signals" can be tracked in relation to solar activity and ionospheric scintillation (e.g. pre–Mar 16 coronal stream, Gamma-Normid window).
- **Three-eye Atlas.** The campaign tracks: (1) 3I/ATLAS capture and Jupiter encounter, (2) Solar / coronal / sun-spot context, (3) Space Cloud Mission Command. Europa signal stream is the **third eye** complement: Jovian-system radio from the same destination (Jupiter/Europa) as the capture.

---

## 3. Scientific context (sources)

- **NenuFAR:** Real-time detection of Solar and Jovian radio bursts; periodic radio emissions from **Jupiter-moon interactions** (including Europa) identified over multi-year data (e.g. Lomb–Scargle; arxiv 2503.18733).
- **Juno:** Jupiter auroral broadband kilometric (bKOM) radio; Europa flyby (Sep 2022) characterized ice shell, not radio. Jovian radio environment is well observed.
- **Public reports.** Some "Europa radio signals" coverage is speculative or unverified (e.g. "intelligent" or "structured" signals). Our framing: we track **Europa / Jovian-system radio** as a natural-physics stream (Europa–Jupiter interaction, periodic emissions) and correlate it with 3I/ATLAS and Sun spots. If future verified reports differ, we update.
