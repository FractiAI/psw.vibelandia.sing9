## Prompt content for text-to-music AI

- **SING4 catalog:** Tracks are **Layer 0** (lyrics + sheet music/beats placeholder). The **Style** column in catalogs/music/README.md is the prompt ingredient (e.g. “Trance anthem · Dance · Simple repeating · Air pumping · AR4366 red-hot tag”). Combine **Title + Style + mold** (from GOLDEN_BACKDOOR_MUSIC_MOLDS_SNAP.md) to build a full prompt.
- **SING9 T3D ORIGIN 15-track soundtrack:** Stored in `data/golden-backdoor-studios.json`; each track has a `prompt` field for text-to-music AI. Page: [interfaces/golden-backdoor-studios.html](interfaces/golden-backdoor-studios.html). These 15 are the “Hero Jo's Golden Backdoor Studios” T3D ORIGIN tracks (A New Story Arrives, My Magical Workstation, …); different lineup from the Hit Factory dance trance list in SING4.
