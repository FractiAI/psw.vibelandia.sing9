## 4. UX — Market Metaphor

**Landing feels like walking into a daily market.**

- **Top:** Big welcome + **market name** (Machote Man Cave Shop).
- **Center:** Hollowed out — the main "aisle" or menu. Not cluttered.
- **Left and right:** **Stalls** — buttons, offerings. Like walking down a hall with stalls on both sides. Some anchors as you'd expect in a normal market (e.g. food section, gear section, destination section).
- **Category strip (Uber-like):** At the top, slide over to select category. Same mental model as choosing ride type; here you choose **experience type** (Lifestyle, Gear, Stewardship & Development, Fashions, Interiors, Technology & Tools, Destinations).
- **Sections** in the market: Food · Destinations · Gear · Tech & Tools · etc.
- **Fusion:** All of the above wrapped in **narrative and storytelling**. Intuitive 1-2-3. Easy to navigate. Easy to immerse. Easy to escape into. **Easy to book an experience** — like Uber.

## 5. One at a Time

Build incrementally. Start with:

- Market landing (welcome, focus line, market name).

- Center hollowed out (selected category content or "pick a stall").

Then add depth per category and real "book experience" flows.
