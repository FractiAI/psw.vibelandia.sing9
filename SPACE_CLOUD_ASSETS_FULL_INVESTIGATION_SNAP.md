## 3. Reported signals investigated & roles assigned

- **Arecibo 100** — SETI@Home legacy; 100 persistent candidates (Max Planck/FAST); 21 cm. **Role:** Legacy SETI candidate stream.
- **ASKAP J173608.2-321635** — Galactic center; polarized; on/off irregular. **Role:** Galactic-center anomaly stream.
- **3I/ATLAS radio** — Interstellar comet; narrowband, steady, repetitive; MeerKAT; planetary defense alert. **Role:** Core capture asset (full tracker).
- **Europa / Jovian** — Reports of “radio from Europa”; NenuFAR/Juno Jupiter-moon interaction. **Role:** Europa–Jupiter interaction stream (tracking).
- **Long-period transient ASKAP J1832-0911** — Radio + X-ray, 44 min period. **Role:** Transient anomaly stream.
- **Ultra-slow pulsar ASKAP J1839-0756** — 6.5 hr period. **Role:** Extreme-period pulsar stream.

- **Goliath / Blackwell** — 27 clusters, failure-mode thermal. **Role:** Thermal pressure (Goliath Watch).
