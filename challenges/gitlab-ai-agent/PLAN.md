## Concept: SING9 HIVE as a GitLab Duo Agent

Build the SING!9 A2A hive as a **GitLab Duo Agent Platform custom agent** — an AI that operates inside GitLab to perform autonomous A2A operations: prospecting, building, submitting, and closing deals — all triggered from GitLab CI/CD pipelines.

- We have a working hive — we're adapting it, not starting from scratch
