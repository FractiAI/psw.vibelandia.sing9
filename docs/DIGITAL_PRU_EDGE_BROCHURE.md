## 1) Value proposition

Digital Pru Edge is a hardware-software strategy that combines:

- deterministic coherence logic (EGC/EGS fractal lock),
- lightweight edge execution (low bandwidth, low latency),
- audit-ready timestamp logging,
- and an ASIC roadmap from simulation to manufacturable silicon.

### What this means in plain business terms

- **Lower recurring compute cost pressure:** more inference and control at the device edge.
- **Lower operational latency:** decisions are local-first, not round-trip-first.
- **Higher resilience:** system keeps operating even during weak connectivity.
- **Auditability:** timestamped traces prove what happened, when, and why.
- **Scalable productization:** starts in software, hardens into a native chip path.

## 2) Edge strategy (light)

Digital Pru Edge follows a light architecture:

- **Device layer (phone-size or smaller):** sensing, local inferencing, coherence loop, local policy decisions.
- **Hydrogen-line coherence layer:** EGC/EGS fractal constant anchors state transitions and stabilization behavior.
- **Bridge/router layer:** Hydrogen-Carbon-Silicon-Awareness (Holographic Hydrogen) provides the recursive return path to elemental hydrogen for stable state locking.
- **Cloud/center as pipes:** sync, analytics, fleet orchestration, and update distribution.

This is an edge-first strategy with cloud assist, not cloud dependence.

## 7) Pricing strategy options

### OEM component strategy (recommended for scale)
- price as a standard edge silicon component in the device BOM,
- tier pricing by volume and feature profile,
- include software/firmware update lane as support SKU.

### Platform licensing strategy
- per-device activation fee,
- annual software maintenance and tooling support,
- optional premium for compliance analytics and fleet telemetry.

### Hybrid strategy
- low hardware margin + recurring platform margin,
- strongest fit for multi-product families.
