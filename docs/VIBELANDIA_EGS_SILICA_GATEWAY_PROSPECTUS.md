> The glass demonstrates in the lab what the cosmos already operates at scale — not a fantasy about future hardware, but a **scale-invariant coupling** anchored in **K_EGS**.

The **EGS Fractal Constant** used in the simulation (from upstream README) is:

```
K_EGS = φ × (λ_laser / λ_H-alpha)
      = 1.6180 × (1030 nm / 656.28 nm)
```

Where **φ** is the golden ratio, **1030 nm** aligns with Nd:glass femtosecond writing into fused silica, and **656.28 nm** is hydrogen-alpha — tying optical glass processing to hydrogen’s optical fingerprint.
