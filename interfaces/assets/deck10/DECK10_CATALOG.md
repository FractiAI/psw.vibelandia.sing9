## THE TREASURE CONCEPT

Deck 6 covered Montana and Alaska — the cold, vast, serious geographic chapters. Deck 10 is the complement: the warm water. The Caribbean, the Pacific tropics, Puerto Rico, the south. The places where the light comes in at an angle that makes everything look already good.

The same characters as always. The same NPCs, the same heroes, the same Pru. But now the water is warm and shallow and turquoise, and the air smells different, and the body moves differently, and the fruit tastes like the tropics, and the rum is cold, and the music is different and the same.

**"El Gran Sol"** is at maximum expression here — the sun that SING!9 references throughout is most literal in the tropics. The tropical chapter is where the solar motif is physical, unavoidable, and everywhere.
