## THE TREASURE CONCEPT

The distinction established in Deck 3 and reinforced in Deck 6 holds here at full expression: **ceremony vs. recreation**. The sacred vs. the carbon version of the same plant.

Marijuana and magic mushrooms appear in the SING!9 universe as **signal amplifiers** — tools for expanding the perception of the signal that is already present. They are not escape. They are approach. The ceremony is not the consumption — the ceremony is the intentional framing that makes the consumption meaningful rather than merely recreational.

The marijuana character (already cataloged in Deck 3, supplemented in Deck 7) is the recurring NPC who appears across geographic chapters holding this duality. Deck 11 expands that character's world into a full treasure deck — showing the ceremonial context, the sacred relationship with the plant, the difference between the person who brings ceremony to it and the person who brings party to it.

**The Psilocybin dimension:** Magic mushrooms are the second substance in this deck — deeper, slower, more architectural. If marijuana opens a window, psilocybin opens the entire wall. In the SING!9 universe, the mushroom journey is the equivalent of telescoping fully open on a node — you go all the way in and the whole universe is in the thing you're looking at.
