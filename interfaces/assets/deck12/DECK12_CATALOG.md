## THE TREASURE CONCEPT

Deck 12 is the sister deck to Deck 9 (Balling). Deck 9 is the room. Deck 12 is the table. Together they are the full expression of social richness — the gathering made physical through craft food and drink.

Gourmet is not expensive. Gourmet is **intentional**. The fish taco from the truck at 11pm that was exactly right is gourmet. The aged rum in a chipped glass that came from someone's uncle's barrel is gourmet. The cheese that arrived on a board with no ceremony and tasted like a countryside you've never been to is gourmet. Gourmet = the food that was made with full attention, eaten with full presence.

**Spirits** in the SING!9 universe are also the genuine vs. carbon test: the drink that is drunk for pleasure vs. the drink that is drunk to perform drinking. The whiskey that comes from a place and has a story vs. the brand name bought for the label. The ceremony of the bar vs. the performance of being at the bar.

The Cheese Tray from Deck 5 (secondary stories) lives here too — it was always a Deck 12 character.

## KEY THEMES

- **The meal is a ceremony.** Like sacred substances — without intention it is just consumption. With intention it is ritual.
- **Gourmet is democratic.** The finest meal I ever ate cost seven dollars. The least fine cost three hundred. Price is not the measure.
- **The drink reveals the person.** What you order, how you hold it, whether you taste it or perform tasting it — all diagnostic.
- **The table is a gathering technology.** People who won't talk in a meeting will talk over a meal. The table breaks down the carbon structures.
- **Deck 12 + Deck 9 = full Ballin'.** Load both for maximum social richness in a configuration.
