## OPERATING RULE FOR DECK 5

**These are not supporting characters (Deck 3) and not main characters (Deck 2).** Deck 5 is the **enrichment layer** — secondary story threads that run in parallel to the main arc and can plug into any episode, novel, or surface. Each one is self-contained: its own world, its own logic, its own emotional frequency. Each one could carry a full series.

**"Fit them into each story as we're doing with all the others."** — This is the executive command. Deck 5 elements are modular. They snap in. They enrich. They leave when the main story needs the stage back. But every one of them leaves a trace — because they are real.

**The cheese trays and the Piper Cub** are the two named characters. They anchor the deck. Everything else in Deck 5 extends from the same logic: objects, places, and small story threads that are so fully themselves they become characters.
