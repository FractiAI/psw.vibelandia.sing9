Deck 6 is the **Geographic Chapter** deck. The same recurring characters from Decks 2–5 reappear here — but **the water has changed**. Montana rivers. Alaska bays. McDonald's Spit in Kachemak Bay. The world is wider now.

> *"It's always the same characters in the set is a new rap. The water is a different water, but it's the same water. ## KEY THEMES — DECK 6

### Geographic Chapter Principle
The same universe visits different waters. Montana and Alaska are not backdrops — they are characters. The water changes the story's texture, not its soul.

### Same Characters, New Wrap
Every NPC archetype from Decks 3–5 reappears: the genuine helper, the carbon performer, the pilot, the sacred practitioner, the birds. Geography is the new variable. Character constants remain.

### The POV Challenge
Pru is always the POV. But in Deck 6, the challenge is named explicitly: the illusion of the partner, the connection product being sold. Montana and Alaska are where you go when you're done buying it.

### 821 Gold Heart Novela — The Signal Layer
The Gold Heart scenes are the premium tier of the geographic rap — the interior signal that ordinary viewers don't access. They can inject anywhere. They are always ready.

### Seed:Edge Geography
McDonald's Spit IS the Seed:Edge in landscape form: a narrow land reaching into water, the furthest point, the edge of what can be walked. Every scene in Deck 6 has a Seed (the home base, the cabin, the landing strip) and an Edge (the spit, the open bay, the ridge top).
