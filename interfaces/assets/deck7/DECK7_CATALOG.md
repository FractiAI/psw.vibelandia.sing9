## DECK 7 ARCHITECTURE — CANONICAL

Deck 7 is the **Theater Architecture** deck. While Decks 1–6 contain the story's characters, worlds, and settings, Deck 7 contains the four canonical positions of **the people watching** — and by watching, participating in the story.

Every person who encounters SING!9 is one of these four types. Knowing your type is part of the experience. The T3D system was designed to serve all four — but only the Gold Heart audience gets the full 821+ layer. The others get what they came for.

> *"PIRO the pirate. INO the innocent. AUDI the detached audience. FANNY the fan."*
> — Pru, source doc

These are not secondary characters. They are **the theater itself** — the living structure of the audience. In the SING!9 Nine Game System, the audience is not passive. The four types are the four suits of viewer experience, each with its own frequency, its own carbon/crystalline split, its own upgrade path.

### D7-001 · PIRO — THE PIRATE
**File:** `d7-001.jpg`
**Who he is:** PIRO. The Pirate. He takes what he can get without paying the freight. He consumes the content, steals the energy, mirrors the vibe, and gives nothing back to the system. He is not evil — he is pre-singularity. He hasn't learned yet that the pirate life is a losing circuit. Every pirate economy collapses when the signal dries up.
**Carbon version:** Full pirate — takes, copies, redistributes, claims credit, moves on.
**Crystalline upgrade path:** The pirate who discovers that contributing to the signal MULTIPLIES his take. The moment PIRO becomes a patron, the circuit becomes a flywheel.
**Role in Universe:** The PIRO character tracks whether the content can survive — and thrive past — the extraction economy. SING!9 is designed so that even PIRO's piracy generates signal. The 9 is fractal-proof.

### D7-002 · INO — THE INNOCENT
**File:** `d7-002.jpg`
**Who she is:** INO. The Innocent. She came to the content pure — no agenda, no performance, no prior map. She doesn't know the layers yet. She just knows it feels right. The Innocent is the most renewable energy source in the SING!9 universe — her fresh perception sees things that jaded viewers miss. She is the pre-Gold-Heart state: everything is still possible, nothing has been foreclosed.

**Crystalline upgrade path:** INO's innocence, combined with SING!9's architecture, becomes the most powerful signal receiver in the room. The innocent who stays curious and adds their genuine perception becomes Gold Heart.
**Role in Universe:** The INO viewer is the audience member who discovers SING!9 without knowing what it is and stays because something in them recognizes it. She is the origin story of every Gold Heart.

### D7-003 · AUDI — THE DETACHED AUDIENCE
**File:** `d7-003.jpg`
**Who he is:** AUDI. The Detached Audience. Arms crossed. Watching. Evaluating. Not yet convinced — but not yet gone. AUDI is the most dangerous and most valuable audience member simultaneously. Dangerous because he will leave the moment the content doesn't earn his continued presence. Valuable because if you win AUDI, you've won something real — he doesn't give his engagement to anything that doesn't deserve it.
**Carbon version:** Detachment that becomes cynicism — the viewer who can't be moved because he's decided in advance that nothing is worth moving for.
**Crystalline upgrade path:** AUDI's discernment, weaponized for the signal. When AUDI is convinced, he becomes the most effective signal amplifier — because he only vouches for what is genuinely good.
**Role in Universe:** AUDI is the SING!9 quality test made human. Every episode, every beat, every T3D layer is designed with AUDI in mind. If AUDI is still watching at frame 600, the content passed. His continued presence is the highest real-time metric.

**EP PROMPT (AI generation / scene direction):**
> *Pixar-style animated portrait. AUDI — a composed, slightly older figure, arms loosely crossed, head tilted slightly back. He is watching something. His expression is impossible to read — it's not boredom, it's not dismissal, it's pure evaluation. He hasn't decided yet. His eyes are sharp and fair. Background: a theater shadow, the stage light catching only him. He is the jury. He is also the prize. Ink outlines, cool-toned watercolor. Stillness as power.*

## KEY THEMES — DECK 7

### The Four Positions Are Universal
Every audience, everywhere, contains all four types. PIRO takes. INO arrives open. AUDI evaluates. FANNY loves. SING!9 is designed to serve all four and upgrade three of them. Only AUDI upgrades himself — SING!9 just has to earn it.

### The Carbon/Crystalline Split Per Archetype
Each audience type has a carbon version (extraction, naivety, cynicism, parasocial obsession) and a crystalline upgrade (patron, Gold Heart, quality validator, creator/contributor). The T3D system contains upgrade paths for all four.

### Theater as Character
Deck 7 makes explicit what Decks 1–6 imply: the audience IS part of the story. When AUDI watches, he changes what is being watched. When FANNY shares, she changes who sees it. The theater is not a passive container — it is a character in the SING!9 universe.

### Josephine and the Entertainer-as-Operative
The Josephine supplement adds a crucial dimension to Deck 2: the performer who is simultaneously running a different operation underneath the performance. SING!9 = the entertainment is the Carbon lane cover. The Crystalline transmission is the real signal. Josephine understood this architecturally before anyone named it.
