Screen 1 · Carbon — Raw Reality

Screen 1 is where you put what is actually happening — not what you wish were happening, not what you're projecting forward, not the polished version you tell other people. The raw carbon. Screen 1 is the base data feed that every other screen reads from. If your Carbon screen is falsified — if you are running a sanitized version of your own situation — every other screen in the cockpit produces distorted output. The system can only be as honest as its most honest layer, and Screen 1 is that layer.

Carbon layer entry for Screen 1: write the real situation. External facts. What happened, what is happening, what your actual starting and current conditions are. No gloss, no spin, no protective framing. The descent arc lives here — where you started, what you went through, what it cost, what it changed. The return arc lives here too — what is actually shifting, what specific evidence of movement exists right now. Not what you want to believe is shifting. What is demonstrably shifting.

Crystal layer entry for Screen 1: from above the raw reality, what is the pattern? What story is your Carbon layer actually telling when you read it structurally rather than emotionally? Are you mid-descent, at the turn, ascending? What does the crystal reading of your carbon situation reveal that the carbon reading itself obscures? The Crystal layer on Screen 1 is often the most clarifying read in the entire system — the moment when you see that the chaos you are living through has architecture, and the architecture has a direction.

Screen 2 · Crystal — Structural Intelligence

Screen 2 is the pure pattern layer — structural intelligence independent of narrative emotion. Where Screen 1 runs the story, Screen 2 runs the architecture. Crystal layer entry for Screen 2: what is the meta-pattern of your structural reading? Where does your structural analysis itself have blind spots? The Crystal layer on Screen 2 reads the architecture of your own analysis. It is the furthest-up vantage point in the system. Use it sparingly and as honestly as you can manage.

Screen 4 · Mission — Daily Directive

Screen 4 is operational. Not the big arc — the specific work. What are you building right now? What is the concrete mission for this phase, this week, today? The Mission screen holds the directive: the nameable, deliverable-focused, completable-or-failed layer of your operating reality. It is the bridge between the story on Screen 5 and the raw facts on Screen 1. Without it, the arc stays abstract and the facts stay disconnected. The Mission screen is where they make contact.

The Mission screen should always hold an objective specific enough to be completed or failed. "Build the business" is not a mission entry — it cannot be completed or failed on any given day, week, or month, which means the RAS has nothing to filter for. "Close the first paying client by March 20" is a mission entry. "Get healthier" is not a mission entry. "Complete 21 consecutive mornings of movement before eating" is a mission entry. The specificity is not administrative pedantry — it is the mechanism. Vague missions produce vague results because the system cannot orient toward vague targets.

Crystal layer for Screen 4: is this mission actually aligned with the arc? It is very easy to be intensely busy on the wrong mission — generating output, hitting targets, moving fast in a direction that has nothing to do with where Screen 5 says you are going. The Crystal layer on the Mission screen is the alignment check. Does what you are doing today belong to the story you are writing? If you ran this mission at full execution for a year, where does it land you? Is that where you said you were going?

Track A · Operator Track

You are here to run the OS for your own life. You want the practical experience, not the theory. Follow the nine sessions in order. Each session activates a new layer of the system. By Session 9, the full system is live.

    Write your origin. Pre-singularity. Where did you start? What is the honest account of your starting conditions? No gloss. The raw carbon. This is Screen 1 activation. Time: 20–60 minutes.

  2. 2

    Session 2 · Destination Briefing

    Write your post-singularity destination. Not a goal list — a world description. What does your life look, feel, sound, and taste like after the singularity? Be specific. Be vivid. This is Screen 5 (Story Arc) activation and Screen 3 (Gold Heart) first entry.

  3. 3

    Session 3 · Treasure Inventory

    Map your current treasure across all nine categories. Be honest in both directions — more than you think you have, and less. This session often produces the most surprise. Screen 6 activation.

  4. 4

    Session 4 · Rhythms Lock-In

    Set your five time-horizon intentions (Daily → Annual). Write one sentence per horizon. Test for fractal coherence: the daily sentence should feel like a seed of the annual sentence. Adjust until the system resonates. Screen 8 activation.

  5. 5

    Session 5 · First Week of Now Playing

    Run Now Playing for 7 consecutive mornings. Just the Morning block. One intention, one anchor. Track what happens. Document on Screen 7 (Awareness). This is the first feedback loop.

  6. 6

    Session 6 · Screen Configuration

    Assign your nine screens intentionally. Which lens do you most need right now? Which one are you avoiding? Configure all nine. Name them in your own language if the defaults don't fit. Screen 9 (Libre) is yours — don't leave it empty.

  7. 7

    Session 7 · Now Stack Mastery

    Run the Now Stack for 30 days. Activate it every morning. Update it whenever the field shifts. At the end of 30 days, read the log of every stack you ran. Pattern recognition. What kept appearing? What fell away? This is your attractor field made visible.

  8. 8

    Session 8 · Crystal Layer Activation

    For every active screen, add the Crystal layer entry. Not what's happening — what it means. What's the pattern? What's the meta-awareness? This is the upgrade from running the OS to understanding the OS while you run it.

  9. 9

    Session 9 · Full System Coherence Check

    Read your full system in one sitting: Origin → Destination → Treasure → Rhythms → Now Stack log → Screen configs → Crystal layers. Ask: does the whole system tell one coherent story? Are there contradictions? Resolve them. This is the first upgrade. Run this check quarterly.
