Look at the Sun

Each month we count: Git commits on two repos, new SoundCloud tracks from the RSS feed, and minutes of audio from episode lengths. We line that up with sunspots and F10.7 — the monthly number NOAA publishes for 10.7 cm solar flux. F10.7 is the usual stand-in for how much EUV is hitting the ionosphere, so it moves with the slow solar cycle much like sunspots.

Charts and tables load from a saved snapshot on this site (look-at-the-sun-study.json). This page does not call GitHub, SoundCloud, or NOAA in your browser.
