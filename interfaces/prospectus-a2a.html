Autonomous email outreach may trigger spam filters or regulatory scrutiny in certain jurisdictions (GDPR, CAN-SPAM). Mitigation: Resend API with proper list hygiene and opt-out compliance. All outreach protocol-governed by NSPFRNP.

If major platforms (Salesforce, MSFT) rapidly commoditize A2A tooling, standalone pipelines may face margin compression. Mitigation: NSPFRNP protocol moat; protocol licensing is the primary revenue target, not commodity agent execution.
