VIBELAND monetizes across three tiers — from zero-cost entry (pop-up games, free) to ultra-premium Baller V experiences ($12,500+). The model converts curious players into Vibers, Vibers into Ballers, Ballers into Franchise nodes.

Three Revenue Tiers
Carbon → Gold → Crystalline monetization

Carbon Layer (free / low-cost): Pop-up games. Entry-level experiences. Wednesdays mixer ($416 incl. gratuity). Free to attend some events. This is the on-ramp — cast the widest net, keep only the Golden Hearts.

Gold Layer (premium): Third Saturdays Baller Crawler ($1K–$5K range). Private Baller V events. Game franchise licensing. Vendor referral fees (10% finders).
