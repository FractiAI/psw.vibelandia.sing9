Executive summary

The upstream project turns a Project Silica–style fused-silica simulation into an EGS Gateway: a bridge between solar–hydrogen–cosmic signals and digital AI logic, with FDTD verification, an EGS OS kernel, and HHAAIOS writer/reader/verifier flows.
